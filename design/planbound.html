    Why the
    boundary matters.
    AI coding agents can make infrastructure changes quickly, but speed changes the review problem. The question is no longer only whether Terraform is valid. It is whether the proposed change still matches the job a human actually authorized.
    PlanBound treats that human intent as a first-class boundary. The task is confirmed before execution, the resulting plan is evaluated change by change, and the apply gate stays closed when a prohibited or unresolved change remains.
    “The agent can propose. The boundary decides what may proceed.”
    The goal is not to replace the reviewer with another layer of AI. It is to make the relationship between intent and infrastructure explicit enough that a reviewer can understand the decision and audit it later.
